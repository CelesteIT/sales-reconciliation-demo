How it works

01 · PULL

Platform orders

Order exports from each delivery platform are pulled on a schedule (API or CSV drop) and normalised into one format.

02 · READ

Odoo sales orders

Read-only XML-RPC calls fetch sale.order records for the same window, batched and filtered to keep load on the ERP low.

03 · MATCH

Reconcile

Orders are matched on the platform reference stored in Odoo, then checked for amount and status. Anything that fails a rule becomes an exception.

04 · ALERT

Act on it

Open exceptions show here, and email alerts go out when the match rate drops or orders go missing, so the team fixes them the same day.
